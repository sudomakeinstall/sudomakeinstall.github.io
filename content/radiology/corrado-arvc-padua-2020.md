## Right Ventricle

### Morpho-functional Ventricular Abnormalities (Category I)

**Major:** Regional RV akinesia, dyskinesia, or bulging, plus ≥1 of:
- Global RV dilatation (increased RV EDV by imaging-specific nomograms)
- Global RV systolic dysfunction (reduced RV EF by imaging-specific nomograms)

**Minor:** Regional RV akinesia, dyskinesia, or aneurysm of RV free wall

NB: Athlete-specific nomograms should be used when appropriate.

### Structural Myocardial Abnormalities (Category II)

**Major:** Transmural LGE (stria pattern) of ≥1 RV region (inlet, outlet, apex) in 2 orthogonal views

## Left Ventricle

### Morpho-functional Ventricular Abnormalities (Category I)

**Minor:**
- Global LV systolic dysfunction (reduced LV EF or reduced global longitudinal strain), with or without LV dilatation (increased LV EDV by imaging-specific nomograms for age, sex, and BSA)
- Regional LV hypokinesia or akinesia of free wall, septum, or both

### Structural Myocardial Abnormalities (Category II)

**Major:** LV LGE (stria pattern) of ≥1 Bull's Eye segment(s) in 2 orthogonal views of the free wall (subepicardial or midmyocardial), septum, or both (excluding septal junctional LGE)

NB: Junctional (i.e. hinge-point) enhancement is specifically excluded.

## Diagnostic Confidence

### ARVC (Dominant Right Variant)

Requires absence of LV involvement. ≥1 morpho-functional and/or structural criterion required at each confidence level.

| Confidence | Criteria |
|------------|----------|
| Definite | 2 major OR 1 major + 2 minor OR 4 minor from different categories |
| Borderline | 1 major + 1 minor OR 3 minor from different categories |
| Possible | 1 major OR 2 minor from different categories |

NB: This table is identical to that used in the 2010 task force criteria.

### Biventricular Variant

Definite/borderline/possible as above, with ≥1 morpho-functional and/or structural abnormality of **both** RV and LV.

### ALVC (Dominant Left Variant)

Structural (± morpho-functional) LV abnormalities + ACM-causing gene mutation, in the absence of RV involvement.
