## Coronary CTA

| Presentation | Scenario | Class |
|--------------|----------|-------|
| Acute | Intermediate risk, no known CAD, no prior testing | 1 |
| Acute | Intermediate risk, no known CAD, prior inconclusive or mildly abnormal stress test ≤1 yr | 2a |
| Acute | Intermediate risk, known nonobstructive CAD (<50% stenosis) | 2a |
| Stable | Intermediate risk, no known CAD | 1 |
| Stable | Known nonobstructive CAD | 2a |
| Stable | Known obstructive CAD, high risk or frequent angina, prior CABG or stents >3mm | 2a |
