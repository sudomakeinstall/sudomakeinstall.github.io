## Shunt Lesions

- Hemodynamically significant shunt: Qp:Qs ≥1.5 with associated chamber enlargement. *(p. e148)*
- PAH: mean PA pressure >20 mm Hg at rest, PVR >2 Wood units, and pulmonary arterial wedge pressure ≤15 mm Hg. *(p. e148)*

### ASD Subtypes *(p. e149)*

| Subtype                        | Frequency |
|--------------------------------|-----------|
| Secundum                       | 80%       |
| Primum (partial AVSD)          | 15%       |
| Sinus venosus, superior        | 5%        |
| Unroofed coronary sinus        | 1%        |
| Sinus venosus, inferior        | <1%       |

## Pulmonic Regurgitation Grading

| Grade    | Regurgitant Fraction | Regurgitant Volume |
|----------|----------------------|--------------------|
| Mild     | <20%                 | <30 mL             |
| Moderate | 20%–40%              | 30–59 mL           |
| Severe   | >40%                 | ≥60 mL             |
