- 4146 outpatients referred to cardiology for stable chest pain, randomized to:
  - 2073 standard of care plus CTA
  - 2073 standard of care
- Primary end point: composite of CHD death or nonfatal MI at 5 yrs
- Median follow-up was 4.8 yrs
- The primary end point was lower in the CTA group: 2.3% vs 3.9%
- Similar rates of invasive coronary angiography (ICA) at 5 yrs: 23.6% vs 24.2%
- Similar rates of revascularization at 5 yrs: 13.5% vs 12.9%
- More preventive therapies initiated in the CTA group: 19.4% vs 14.7%, OR 1.40
- More antianginal therapies initiated in the CTA group: 13.2% vs 10.7%, OR 1.27
- Though overall rates of ICA and revascularization were similar, rates beyond 12 months were higher in the standard of care group
- Risk thresholds for initiation of preventive therapy have fallen since the trial was completed
