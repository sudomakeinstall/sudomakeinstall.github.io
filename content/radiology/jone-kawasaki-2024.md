## Coronary Z-Score Calculation

Guidelines use z-scores calculated from Pediatric Heart Network data:

- [Calculator](https://www.pediatricheartnetwork.org/z-scores-calculator/)
- [Formulas](https://www.pediatricheartnetwork.org/forresearchers/echo-z-scores/)

## Grading

| Z-score                            | Grading        |
|------------------------------------|----------------|
| <2                                 | Normal         |
| 2–2.5                              | Dilated        |
| 2.5–<5                             | Small          |
| 5–<10 and absolute dimension <8 mm | Medium         |
| ≥10 or absolute dimension >8 mm    | Large or giant |
