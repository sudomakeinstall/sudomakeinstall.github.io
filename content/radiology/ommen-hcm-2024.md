## Diagnostic Thresholds

- Adults
  - Maximal ED wall thickness ≥15 mm (in the absence of another cause)
  - Maximal ED wall thickness ≥13 mm (with a pathologic gene or a family history)
- Children
  - Z-score ≥2.5 (asymptomatic, no family history)
  - Z-score ≥2 (with a pathologic gene or a family history)

## Imaging Risk Factors for Sudden Cardiac Death

- Massive LVH
  - Adults: ≥30 mm (or ≥28 mm in some patients)
  - Children: Z-score ≥20 (or ≥10 in some patients)
- EF <50%
- Apical aneurysm with associated LGE
- "Extensive" LGE: ≥15% of myocardial mass

## Morphologic Features

- Wall thickening in the basal anteroseptal and anterior segments most common
- Hypertrophied and apically displaced papillary muscles
- Anomalous insertion of the papillary muscle directly in the anterior MV leaflet (no chordae tendineae)
- Elongated MV leaflets
- Myocardial crypts
- Myocardial bridging
- RV hypertrophy

## Obstruction

- ≥30 mm Hg: LVOT obstruction present
- ≥50 mm Hg: Threshold for treatment

- Must distinguish between different types of obstruction:
  - SAM (may be dynamic, due to an elongated leaflet, or chordal)
  - Fixed subvalvular
  - Midcavitary obliteration (may be due to hyperdynamic function or abnormal/hypertrophied papillary muscles)

## Genetics

- Autosomal dominant
- Genes affecting the sarcomere
  - Beta-myosin heavy chain 7: MYH7
  - Myosin binding protein C3: MYBPC3
  - Rare: TNNI3, TNNT2, TPM1, MYL2, MYL3, ACTC1

## Differential Diagnosis

- Remodeling from longstanding hypertension
- Athlete's heart
- Amyloid
- Sarcoid
- Fabry (gene: GLA)
- Danon (gene: LAMP2)
- Glycogen storage disease cardiomyopathy (PRKAG2)
- Other glycogen and lysosomal storage diseases in children
- RASopathies
- Mitochondrial myopathies
- Friedreich's ataxia
