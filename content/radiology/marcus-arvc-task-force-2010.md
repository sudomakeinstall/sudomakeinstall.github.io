## Cardiac MRI (Category I)

Regional RV akinesia, dyskinesia, or dyssynchronous contraction, plus ≥1 of:

| Criterion | Major | Minor |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| RVEDV/BSA (male) | ≥ 110 mL/m² | 100–110 mL/m² |
| RVEDV/BSA (female) | ≥ 100 mL/m² | 90–100 mL/m² |
| RVEF | ≤ 40% | 40–45% |

NB: As pointed out in the Padua criteria, these cutoffs are based on fast gradient echo sequences which predate SSFP and are known to underestimate volumes.

## Diagnostic Confidence

| Confidence | Criteria |
|------------|----------|
| Definite | 2 major OR 1 major + 2 minor OR 4 minor from different categories |
| Borderline | 1 major + 1 minor OR 3 minor from different categories |
| Possible | 1 major OR 2 minor from different categories |
