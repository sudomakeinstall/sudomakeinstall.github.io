## LV Volumetry

### Male

| Parameter | Mean±SD | Range | Mild | Moderate | Severe |
|-----------|---------|-------|------|----------|--------|
| LVM/BSA (g/m²) | 55 ± 8 | 39–72 | 73–80 | 81–88 | >88 |
| LVEDV/BSA (mL/m²) | 75 ± 14 | 49–102 | 103–116 | 117–130 | >130 |
| LVEF (%) | 64 ± 7 | 51–77 | 44–50 | 37–43 | <37 |

### Female

| Parameter | Mean±SD | Range | Mild | Moderate | Severe |
|-----------|---------|-------|------|----------|--------|
| LVM/BSA (g/m²) | 45 ± 6 | 33–56 | 57–62 | 63–68 | >68 |
| LVEDV/BSA (mL/m²) | 68 ± 11 | 46–90 | 91–101 | 102–112 | >112 |
| LVEF (%) | 66 ± 6 | 54–79 | 48–53 | 42–47 | <42 |

NB:
- Papillary muscles/trabeculations included in the LV volume.
- Severity tiers one standard deviation wide.
- Adapted from Table 3 of the reference.
