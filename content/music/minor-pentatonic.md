# Minor Pentatonic Scale

The minor pentatonic scale consists of 5 notes: 1, ♭3, 4, 5, and ♭7.
The E minor pentatonic scale is shown below as an example.
The ♭5 is commonly added as a chromatic passing tone between the 4 and 5, known as the "blue note."
Adding this note creates the blues scale.
The ♭5 is shown on the fretboard below but is not included in the box patterns.
